No candidate demonstrates a robust advantage across both historical vintages.
This is historical exploratory research on already-inspected broad-market data,
with newly inspected sector histories; it is not blind future validation.

252 matured labels, and 21-bar refits are unchanged. Compare full four-feature
model and three frozen two-feature ablations against calibrated current-volatility
level. The study tests risk probabilities, not direction or investment returns.

## Inference and model simplification

96 primary paired Brier tests (6 markets x 2 vintages x 2 populations x 4
candidates vs level); 72 secondary ablation-vs-full tests, separately Holm
corrected. Neither family has a p<.05 corrected result with block length20.
Bootstrap approximate stationarity remains an assumption, not a validation certificate.

The initial 999-draw inference was numerically too coarse for 96 tests: minimum
p=.001 implies minimum first Holm p=.096. After seeing initial scores, all tests
and block sensitivities were rerun uniformly with 9999 draws. Initial evidence
is preserved. Model parameters, dates, predictions and hypotheses did not change.
This post-score inference amendment is disclosed; no claims of preregistered blind
validation are made. Monte Carlo uncertainty matters near .05.

S&P recent full skill +14.64% has raw p=.0006 and Holm p=.0576 in this NEW
96-test family. The earlier 36-test experiment remains preserved and had a
corrected positive result; a different family changes multiplicity inference,
not the predictions. Old S&P full p=.0643, and recent calendar2023 skill -35.23%.
Acceleration-only gives +5.45% old and +5.64% recent versus level, but does not
establish corrected evidence or a stable improvement over full.
TSX acceleration-only improves recent skill to +1.13% versus full +.57%, but old
skill decreases to +3.48% versus full +3.82%; this is no demonstrated improvement.
No ablation consistently improves the full model with corrected evidence.

Canadian technology: full recent skill +10.26%, raw p=.0017, Holm p=.153;
old skill +1.41%, p=.3714. Recent calm-origin skill is only +1.81%, p=.5188.
Recent annual all-origin skills:2022 +6.60%,2023 +13.42%,2024 +10.48%,2025
+10.46%,2026 +12.79% (partial boundary years). All ten non-overlap offsets are
positive (+5.80% to +15.29%). Leaving each year out gives +9.64% to +11.30%.
These are encouraging descriptive stability checks, but old offsets include
negative values, old years are unstable, and onset-from-calm evidence is weak.
Recent average probability .2397 versus event rate .2362; this mean agreement
alone does not prove calibration. Probability-bin diagnostics are included.
Technology is a hypothesis for future testing, not a validated forecasting edge.

Financials, energy and materials fail to carry old improvements into recent data.
Energy recent average probability .1921 versus observed event rate .2414 flags
underprediction. No sector-specific retuning or winner promotion was performed.
