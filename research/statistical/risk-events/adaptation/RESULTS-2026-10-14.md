# Fixed training-window and probability calibration tests — 2026-10-14

## Decision

Neither fixed modification establishes a repeatable improvement over the original
expanding model. Retain the original model as a historical candidate; do not
promote a variant. This is historical exploratory analysis, NOT blind validation.
Negative measured skills are descriptive; absence of corrected significance does
not prove an effect is zero. No prospective collection was activated.

## Bounded research disposition

1. Reject these two variants as upgrades; keep original expanding full and level.
2. Preserve S&P and Canadian technology as exploratory hypotheses only. Their
   earlier positive results are not strengthened by this failed adaptation test.
3. Future validation must record probabilities before label maturity, freeze
   source/target/comparisons and define duration/power and calendar rules BEFORE
   collection. Data revisions and changing ETF adjustments require snapshot policy.
   No scheduler, live signals, operational model or forward monitoring was enabled.
4. A downside-loss/semivariance target is a NEW question. Predefine its economic
   target and competitive baseline separately rather than continue modifying this
   observed high-volatility window until it succeeds. Historical study can be
   exploratory; confirmation needs untouched future outcomes.
