## Execution quality

- Synthetic no-future-features, strictly matured-labels, input integrity and formula checks PASS.
- All 9 data series fetched with no API failures. US/Canada equity indices and ETF proxies: 1,702-1,704 daily bars beginning January 2020 and ending October 13, 2026. BTC/ETH: 2,477 bars beginning January 2020 and ending October 12, 2026 (source's last complete crypto daily bar).

Across 27 asset × horizon tests, 24 have **negative Brier skill**. Only Russell 2000 at 20 bars improves on both Brier (+1.0%) and return MSE (+2.3%), and *does not* improve directional accuracy (52.3% vs 55.3%). This is a weak and exploratory result; do not treat it as validated alpha.

## Implication

The frozen simple price-only regression baseline does **not** provide reliable cross-asset predictive value against basic historic priors on this test. Next independent research phases could add market regimes, macroeconomic factors, and forecast volatility (HAR), but must predefine inputs and reserve a fresh chronological holdout. Do not optimize on this same holdout and later claim it is out-of-sample.

Current model probability forecasts exist in machine-readable artifact, but given negative comparative forecasting skill should NOT be used for allocation or execution. This is intentionally independent from Market Hunter and existing SMC research.
