# Pre-2020 volatility replication — 14 October 2026

## Decision

The proposed broad forecasting advantage did **not** replicate. The unchanged log-RMS ridge loses to EWMA on aggregate h20 volatility MSE in all five equity indices. Several shorter-horizon variance-loss comparisons show statistically detectable inferiority, not superiority. Gold's small MSE improvement reverses the post-2020 sign; silver is essentially tied on aggregate and offset-sensitive. Crypto h20 MSE remains positive, but BTC's older advantage is weaker and ETH's test is short. No h20 QLIKE superiority survives the fixed-family Holm correction.

Do not promote this model to a general cross-asset risk engine or tune the newly examined window into a purported holdout. Freeze the current general-purpose candidate as a failed broad replication. Retain crypto as a narrow exploratory hypothesis, with sample, target and calibration limitations. These results do not prove statistical volatility forecasting itself is useless, or that EWMA has been validated as a portfolio policy.

These are exploratory, assumption-dependent tests with pre-existing researcher selection, not universal declarations of model inferiority.

The candidate underpredicts more often than EWMA in every older market except ETH. For BTC this is 69.4% versus 46.1%. This weakens the claim that positive aggregate crypto volatility MSE alone would make the model a reliable risk-control input. Frequency is only one calibration measure; magnitude and loss preference are retained separately. No portfolio drawdown or investment performance was simulated.

A plausible mechanism is log-target retransformation: exponentiating a log-RMS conditional estimate and squaring it does not generally estimate conditional mean variance. Mean reversion can also shrink rising risk. These mechanisms are hypotheses consistent with the specification and diagnostics, not causal explanations established by this experiment.

## Other fixed benchmarks

The restricted GARCH, mean-reversion and linear HAR-variance alternatives were evaluated without changing their settings. They do not produce a universal winner across both periods, losses and asset classes. Some have better QLIKE but worse volatility MSE. Do not promote a secondary specification chosen after reading these outcomes to an independently validated model. The full private artifact contains all fixed comparisons; aggregated machine-readable results are in `PRE2020-RESULTS-2026-10-14.json`.

## Research disposition

1. Close the claim of a general robust advantage for the current price/direction/macro and log-RMS volatility specification. Preserve the code and all positive/negative records.
2. Keep simple volatility estimates as research comparators. This test does not authorize allocation or production risk sizing with either model.
3. Crypto h20 is a limited hypothesis worth retaining, not a confirmed exception. Further work would need a predeclared target/loss consistent with risk use and genuinely new validation; both historical periods are now inspected and cannot be recycled as new holdouts.
4. No automatic prospective schedule, engine integration, model optimization, portfolio strategy or PR merge was performed.
