# Research v3 results — 14 October 2026

## Assessment

No robust independent forecasting advantage is established. A replayable evidence system and more competitive benchmarks now exist. The existing log-RMS volatility model remains promising for further research, especially crypto, but improvements depend on the loss function and comparator. The strongest apparent crypto QLIKE improvements do not survive Holm adjustment of the fixed 27-test exploratory family at 5%.

This is historical exploratory research on already-inspected data, not blind validation and not a trading or portfolio performance experiment. No production, SMC, Telegram or portfolio integration was changed. PR #74 stays draft and unmerged.

## Implemented audit repairs

- New private evidence directories preserve exact raw response bytes, normalized bars, provider retrieval times, exchange timezone, session completion rules, adjustment provenance, code hashes/source snapshots, fit parameters and dated prediction/target ledgers. Existing directories cannot be overwritten.
- Offline replay verifies file hashes, source hashes, configuration and normalized-bar equality. A separate `--data-bundle` mode explicitly labels changed code on existing source data as a new experiment, with a parent-manifest hash.
- Invalid, signed, missing and duplicate observations cannot be silently compressed away. Empty Yahoo holiday/weekend placeholders are explicitly logged. US and Canadian holidays are checked separately, with DST-aware completion guards and rejection of unexplained missing weekdays. Macro inputs also carry available-at checks.
- The new macro specification identifies USO adjusted prices as an oil ETF proxy instead of applying log returns to a continuous futures series that can be nonpositive. This semantic change was fixed before comparison; it was not chosen for the best historical score.
- Missing required contexts, markets or horizons produce an incomplete run and failure exit code. Failed-response bodies are retained when available.
- Original Actions summary JSON files are preserved byte-for-byte in `../original-evidence/`; original engine, phase2 code and committed result summaries were not edited.

## Fixed additional comparisons

The old log-RMS ridge is unchanged numerically on identical inputs. Benchmarks now include persistence 5/20/60, original expanding mean future RMS, expanding mean future variance, EWMA(.94), a 50/50 mean-reversion baseline, a linear-variance HAR-style model using daily/5/22 squared-close-return averages, and a restricted variance-targeted Gaussian GARCH(1,1).

GARCH is deliberately limited: zero mean, omega tied to the training second moment, a fixed alpha/beta grid fitted to training likelihood, and no tuning on future outcomes. It is not unrestricted maximum-likelihood GARCH. HAR is based on daily closes, not intraday HAR-RV. Beating these particular implementations is not proof of beating their entire model classes.

The equity/ETF vol20 skill agrees with the original table to reported precision. EWMA is a stronger comparator: positive error reductions for equity indices and crypto persist in the aggregate, while gold and silver lose to EWMA and restricted GARCH. QLIKE is more mixed: S&P 500 and Dow h20 are slightly worse than EWMA even though volatility MSE improves. Gold/silver are worse in both. BTC/ETH h20 QLIKE intervals exclude zero before multiplicity correction, but adjusted p-values are .200/.414; neither is significant in the declared family. No family member meets adjusted p<.05.

TSX illustrates comparator/loss dependence: h20 volatility MSE beats a simple mean-reverting blend by 7.9%, but its QLIKE is worse than that blend. A universal superiority claim would therefore be inaccurate. Statistical rationale: a good level-volatility predictor need not be a good conditional-variance predictor, especially when its log-target estimate is exponentiated and then squared.

## Direction, macro and data diagnostics

The revised macro probability model still worsens Brier score in all 27 matched market/horizon comparisons. Its h20 changes remain approximately -4.8% to -12.7% relative to price-only. This does not identify a causal explanation or establish that macro information is useless. No macro hyperparameters were optimized. Probabilities, fixed reliability bins, log loss, zero-return and daily-updated own-price baselines are recorded.

Logistic gradient diagnostics found no apparent original-optimizer convergence failures in the new vintage: maximum absolute penalized gradient was approximately 4.53e-10, with zero fits above 1e-4. The suspected optimizer problem was not confirmed.

This run encountered 63 empty VIX holiday placeholders and 357 empty DXY holiday/weekend placeholders, all recorded with reasons. Five long TSX Christmas gaps were flagged and match declared Canadian holidays; no unexplained weekday gaps remained. Exact raw bytes and timestamps are in the private evidence bundle. Historical revisions/publication assumptions still prevent this vintage from being point-in-time 2020–2026 data.

## Dependence, calibration and stability diagnostics

The private full summary contains every non-overlap offset, annual absolute losses, leave-one-year-out comparisons, causal regime error tables and episode counts, fixed daily-risk bins, calibration slopes/intercepts and high-risk underprediction frequencies. These tables are descriptive and do not fit regime-conditioned signals or choose an offset.

Paired loss inference uses a fixed-seed circular moving-block bootstrap, 999 resamples, primary block length 2h, sensitivity h and 4h. Approximate stationarity is an assumption; structural changes can limit coverage. Holm correction controls only the declared 27 comparisons subject to valid underlying p-values, not all previously inspected researcher choices. No historical diagnostic is labelled independent confirmatory evidence. Ratio skills are not calculated on additive QLIKE losses, which can be negative.

## Verification

Original synthetic suites pass. New synthetic checks cover original-model numerical parity; complete-path future perturbation including normalization and fitted parameters; label maturity; ridge and GARCH oracles; duplicate/missing/signed/partial-bar rejection; holiday/DST rules; block-bootstrap and Holm arithmetic; required-section completeness; source/input hashes; overwrite and tamper rejection; and network-disabled offline replay. Synthetic fixtures are not market evidence and are removed after testing.

A complete real-data run has nine markets, four external contexts, all horizons and zero recorded errors. Final real-data offline replay reproduced all market metrics, contexts and Holm results exactly; all 36 forecast-ledger and fit-snapshot file SHA256 hashes are identical. The bundle records a dirty working-tree git parent SHA because it was run before publication; its saved source-file hashes and source snapshots identify the actual code used. Changes are restricted to isolated research files and one correctness-only workflow. No raw provider history is committed to the public repository.

## Next research gate

The next scientific question is whether a preselected volatility/variance formulation can outperform simple EWMA and mean-reversion consistently on future data and high-risk events. This version supplies the evidence needed to decide that. It does not justify adding risk controls to existing engines yet.

Before future collection, freeze a new protocol/code version, primary loss and horizon, comparator, observation duration/minimum useful improvement, missing-forecast rules and archive retention. Future model changes require a new version. Historical results cannot select a winner and simultaneously serve as its blind validation. A separate portfolio risk study would additionally need covariance, risk overshoots, costs, exposure limits and turnover.

No prospective schedule or live signals were activated in this work.
