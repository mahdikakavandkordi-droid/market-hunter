Purpose: test whether the unchanged daily-close volatility forecasting advantage generalizes to a different historical period. This is external historical replication of a specification selected after inspecting 2020–2026, NOT genuine prospective blind validation. Direction and macro research are frozen and not evaluated in this replication.

Input request: 1 January 2005 through 31 December 2019, nine original instruments. ETFs and crypto use only actual provider history since their inception/provider availability; no fabricated earlier prices, no symbol substitutions, no pre-inception data. If fewer than the fixed minimum are available, report the instrument unavailable. Each instrument keeps its native trading calendar. The expected later start for SLV, BTC and ETH is disclosed; calendar years are not comparable counts across assets.

Re-estimate every model chronologically from pre-2020 observations alone, beginning with the same minimum 252 fully matured targets, first feature origin t=60 and refit every 21 native bars. Do not import coefficients, normalization or future-price labels from 2020–2026. The model specification/benchmark suite remains unchanged. Do not use this replication to optimize settings.
